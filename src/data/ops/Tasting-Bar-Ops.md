# Tasting Bar Ops

Hours: Thursday–Sunday, 8am–2pm. Small counter, 12 seats, no wifi (intentional).

Menu: espresso, pourover (V60), batch brew, cortado, cappuccino, latte. Milk options: whole, oat, no flavored syrups. Pastries from Loam Bakery daily (see [[Wholesale-Accounts]]).

Staffing: Devon leads, plus 1–2 weekend baristas ([[Hiring-Plan]]). Opening checklist covers grinder calibration, water TDS check (target 150ppm), and pastry receipt.

Average ticket $6.80, ~140 tickets/day peak, dropping ~30% in summer (see seasonality note in [[Cashflow-Risks]]). Equipment listed in [[Equipment-Inventory]].
