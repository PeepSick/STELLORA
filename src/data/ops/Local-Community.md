# Local Community

Neighborhood-first is a core value from [[Business-Overview]]. Ongoing commitments:

- Free coffee for the Woodlawn Elementary teachers' lounge, delivered monthly.
- Host the Southeast Portland Small Business Roundtable, second Tuesday, at the tasting bar (about 20 attendees).
- 5% of tasting-bar Sunday sales go to a rotating local nonprofit — 2024 total donated: $ 4,320.
- Sponsor of the Alberta Street Fair (relevant to future second location, see [[Growth-Strategy]]).
- Employ two apprentice baristas per year from the Portland Roasters Guild youth program.

Ties to [[Marketing-Plan]] but is not marketing — no cross-promotion on donations.
