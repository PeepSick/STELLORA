# stock-market-basics

Stock market indices like the S&P 500 and Dow Jones track the performance of large publicly traded companies. Investment returns depend on risk tolerance, time horizon, and asset allocation. Bonds provide fixed income with lower volatility than stocks. Treasury bonds are backed by the government. Mutual funds and ETFs pool investor money for diversified exposure. Portfolio rebalancing maintains target allocations.
