# Ethiopia Origin

Sourced through Kochere Cooperative in the Yirgacheffe zone via importer Trabocca. Small-holder washing station model — 700+ farmers contribute cherry.

Two lots per year:
- Natural process (Kochere lot #4) — anchor of our lightest [[Single-Origins]] offering, top cupping score ([[Cupping-Notes]]).
- Washed process — 10% component of the [[House-Blend]].

2024 FOB $6.10/lb. Ships from Djibouti, ~10 weeks to Oakland. Coffee arrives June–August.

Risks: Ethiopian export delays, currency volatility. See [[Supply-Chain-Risks]]. Story used heavily in [[Marketing-Plan]] content.
