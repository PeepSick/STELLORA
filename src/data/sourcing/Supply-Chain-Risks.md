# Supply Chain Risks

Top three supply-side risks tracked monthly:

1. C-market price spikes — arabica up 31% YoY. Discussed in [[Cashflow-Risks]]. Mitigated with 6-month forwards on Colombian contracts ([[Colombia-Origin]]).
2. Origin logistics — Ethiopian export delays added 3 weeks to our 2024 [[Ethiopia-Origin]] arrivals. Buffer: keep 8 weeks of green inventory minimum.
3. Single-supplier concentration — 60% of the [[House-Blend]] is one Colombian farm. Backup relationships being developed in Nariño and Cauca departments.

Insurance covers cargo up to $50,000 per shipment. Reviewed with broker in January.
