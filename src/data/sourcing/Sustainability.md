# Sustainability

Zero-waste roasting is a stated value in [[Business-Overview]]. Concrete practices:

- Chaff collected and composted with a partner urban farm (Sauvie Island).
- Reject roasts (about 1.4% of volume, tracked in [[KPIs-Dashboard]]) donated to a local nonprofit for cold brew.
- Retail bags are compostable kraft with a plant-based liner — 22% more expensive than plastic-lined but on-brand.
- Delivery van converted to biodiesel in 2023.
- Solar array on the roastery roof covers ~40% of electricity, installed with a state incentive.

Not yet: carbon offset of green shipping (evaluating), and reusable wholesale bins (piloting with Field Notes Coffee, see [[Wholesale-Accounts]]).
