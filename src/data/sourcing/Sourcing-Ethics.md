# Sourcing Ethics

Every lot we buy meets one of two conditions:
1. Direct trade with a farm we've visited or video-called with, at a price at least 25% above the C-market floor.
2. Purchased through Cafe Imports or Red Fox from a farm with published FOB pricing.

We publish an annual transparency report listing every lot, the FOB price paid, and the importer. 2024 report showed average FOB $4.85/lb vs C-market average of $2.10/lb.

Long-term relationships with Finca La Esperanza (Colombia — see [[Colombia-Origin]]) and Kochere Cooperative (Ethiopia — [[Ethiopia-Origin]]). Ties to [[Business-Overview]] core values and [[Marketing-Plan]] messaging.
