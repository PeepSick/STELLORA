# Colombia Origin

Primary partner: Finca La Esperanza, Pitalito, Huila. Owned by the Muñoz family, third generation. 8 hectares at 1,750 m elevation. Varieties: Caturra, Castillo, Pink Bourbon.

We buy 3,500 lb annually across two harvests (Apr–Jun and Oct–Dec). FOB paid in 2024: $5.20/lb, well above the [[Sourcing-Ethics]] floor. Contract signed in October, arrives at Oakland port in January.

The Huila washed goes into the [[House-Blend]] (60%) and as a standalone [[Single-Origins]] offering. Backup supplier: Cafe Imports' Nariño micro-lot program. Related: [[Supply-Chain-Risks]].
