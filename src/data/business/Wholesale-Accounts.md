# Wholesale Accounts

As of April 2025 we serve 14 active wholesale accounts. Top five by monthly volume:

- Field Notes Coffee (SE Division) — 42 lb/wk of House Blend
- Loam Bakery (NE Alberta) — 28 lb/wk, mixed
- Second Draft (Vancouver WA) — 24 lb/wk
- Rye & Fig (Hawthorne) — 20 lb/wk
- Meridian Hotel — 18 lb/wk, private label

Standard pricing: $14.50/lb for blends, $17/lb for single origin. Net-15 terms (see [[Cashflow-Risks]]). Two accounts (Loam, Rye & Fig) chronically late.
