# Growth Strategy

Three-year plan (2025-2027):
1. Grow [[Wholesale-Accounts]] from 14 to 30 cafes, prioritizing Portland and Vancouver WA.
2. Launch subscription box (see [[Subscription-Launch]]) targeting 400 active subscribers by end of 2026.
3. Open a second tasting bar in the Alberta Arts District by mid-2027, contingent on hitting subscriber targets.

Key risks flagged in [[Cashflow-Risks]] and [[Supply-Chain-Risks]]. Marketing plan lives in [[Marketing-Plan]]. Hiring roadmap in [[Hiring-Plan]].

Not pursuing: grocery retail (margins too thin), franchising (dilutes brand), or export.
