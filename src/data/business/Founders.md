# Founders

Ember & Oak was co-founded by Maya Chen and Rafael Ortiz in March 2021. Maya spent seven years as head roaster at a Seattle roastery and holds a Q Grader certification. Rafael comes from restaurant operations and manages [[Financials-2025]], [[Wholesale-Accounts]], and staffing. They bootstrapped the company with $85,000 in personal savings plus a $60,000 SBA microloan.
