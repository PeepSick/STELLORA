# Founders

Ember & Oak was co-founded by Maya Chen and Rafael Ortiz in March 2021. Maya spent seven years as head roaster at a Seattle roastery and holds a Q Grader certification. Rafael comes from restaurant operations and manages [[Financials-2025]], [[Wholesale-Accounts]], and staffing.

They met at a Specialty Coffee Association event in 2019 and bootstrapped the company with $85,000 in personal savings plus a $60,000 SBA microloan (see [[Financials-2025]]). Ownership is split 55/45 in Maya's favor, with a buy-sell agreement drafted in 2023.

Both founders remain full-time. Long-term succession is discussed in [[Growth-Strategy]].
