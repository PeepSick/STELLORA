# Business Overview

Ember & Oak is a small-batch specialty coffee roastery based in Portland, Oregon. Founded in 2021 by [[Founders]], we source single-origin green beans directly from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Our flagship product is the [[House-Blend]], a medium roast that anchors our wholesale accounts.

We operate a 1,200 sq ft roastery, a small tasting bar open Thursday–Sunday, and an online store shipping nationwide. See [[Financials-2025]] and [[Growth-Strategy]] for direction.

Core values: transparency with farmers ([[Sourcing-Ethics]]), zero-waste roasting ([[Sustainability]]), and neighborhood-first retail ([[Local-Community]]).
