# Cashflow Risks

Top risks to cash position in 2025:

1. Green coffee price volatility. Arabica C-market up 31% year-over-year. Every $0.20/lb move affects annual COGS by ~$14,000. Partial hedge: 6-month forward contracts with our Colombian importer.
2. Wholesale receivables aging — see [[Wholesale-Accounts]]. Two cafes are consistently 45+ days late. Net-15 terms are being enforced starting Q2.
3. Equipment failure. The Probat 12kg roaster is 14 years old. Rebuild cost ~$18,000, replacement ~$65,000. See [[Equipment-Inventory]].
4. Seasonality — tasting bar revenue drops 30% in summer.

Mitigations tracked with Rafael monthly. Related: [[Financials-2025]].
