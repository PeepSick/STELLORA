# Marketing Plan

Positioning: "coffee with a paper trail" — farm-transparent, small-batch, neighborhood roastery. Voice is warm, nerdy, unpretentious.

Channels for 2025:
- Instagram (@emberandoak, 8,400 followers) — 3 posts/wk, focus on roast profiles and farmer stories from [[Sourcing-Ethics]].
- Email newsletter — monthly, 2,100 subscribers, 38% open rate.
- Local partnerships with Loam Bakery ([[Wholesale-Accounts]]) for pop-ups.
- Print: quarterly zine bundled with [[Subscription-Launch]] boxes.

Budget: $1,400/month, mostly content and photography. No paid ads in 2025. Tracking in [[KPIs-Dashboard]].
