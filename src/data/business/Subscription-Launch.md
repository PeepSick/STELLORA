# Subscription Launch

Launching "Ember Monthly" in July 2025. One 12oz bag of rotating single-origin plus a printed tasting card and micro-zine. Two tiers: whole bean $22/mo, ground $22/mo (same price, discourages grinding by removing the discount).

Target: 150 subscribers by end of Q3, 400 by end of 2026 (see [[Growth-Strategy]]).

Fulfillment via Shopify Subscriptions + Shippo. Packaging redesign in progress with local studio Kiln & Co, estimated $4,200 one-time (see [[Financials-2025]]).

Risks: fulfillment labor spike, coffee freshness on ground orders. Team notes in [[Hiring-Plan]]. Marketing hooks in [[Marketing-Plan]].
