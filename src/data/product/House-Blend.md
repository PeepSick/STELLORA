# House Blend

Our flagship. Medium roast, developed by Maya in 2021 and refined twice since. Blend ratio: 60% Colombian Huila (washed), 30% Guatemalan Antigua (washed), 10% Ethiopian Sidamo (natural) for a citrus lift.

Tasting notes: milk chocolate, toasted almond, ripe orange, brown sugar finish. Roast level: City+ (first crack + 45 seconds, drop temp 415°F). Development ratio around 22%.

Accounts for 48% of total volume. Retails $18/12oz bag, wholesale $14.50/lb. See [[Wholesale-Accounts]], [[Roasting-Profiles]]. Sourcing details in [[Sourcing-Ethics]] and [[Colombia-Origin]].
