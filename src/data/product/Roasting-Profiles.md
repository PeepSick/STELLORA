# Roasting Profiles

All profiles managed in Cropster. General framework:

- Charge temperature: 380–405°F depending on bean density.
- Turnaround at 1:30–1:45.
- Yellow phase (drying) target 4:30–5:00.
- First crack between 8:30 and 9:45.
- Development time 90–120 seconds, ratio 18–24% of total time.
- Drop between 405°F (light) and 428°F (medium-dark).

The [[House-Blend]] components are roasted separately and blended post-roast for consistency. Single origins profiled individually — see [[Single-Origins]] and [[Cupping-Notes]]. Weekly calibration cuppings every Friday.
