# Cupping Notes

Cupping protocol follows SCA standard: 8.25g coffee to 150ml water at 200°F, 4-minute steep, break at 4:00, skim, evaluate at 8, 15, 22 minutes.

Weekly Friday cupping evaluates:
- All roasts from the week
- One competitor sample (blind)
- Any new green offer samples from importers

Scored on aroma, flavor, aftertaste, acidity, body, balance, uniformity, clean cup, sweetness, defects, overall. Anything below 84 gets a profile adjustment (see [[Roasting-Profiles]]).

Historical scores tracked in Cropster. The Ethiopian Yirgacheffe from Kochere lot #4 currently scores highest at 88.5.
